Coverage Solutions

Every Coverage Your Trucking Operation Needs

Comprehensive insurance products built specifically for the commercial trucking industry.

Auto Liability

Primary liability coverage required for all CMVs operating on public roads.

Physical Damage

Comprehensive and collision protection for your trucks, tractors, and equipment.

Motor Truck Cargo

Protect the freight you haul against damage, theft, and loss in transit.

General Liability

Business liability coverage for bodily injury and property damage claims.

Non-Trucking Liability

Bobtail coverage for when you're operating under your own dispatch.

Trailer Interchange

Coverage for trailers you pull that belong to another party under interchange agreement.

DOT / MC Filings

BMC-91, BMC-34, MCS-90 filings handled to keep your authority active and compliant.

Workers Compensation

Required coverage for your drivers and employees in most operating states.
